## The intersection nobody asked for

Most finance students are learning DCF models. I'm building Chrome extensions that auto-fill forms with AI.

It started when I got tired of repeating the same information across 50 internship applications. The inefficiency was painful — so I built a tool to fix it.

## Finance thinking meets engineering

Finance teaches you to think in systems. Cash flows, risk models, portfolio theory — it's all about understanding how pieces connect and where value compounds.

Building AI tools uses the same mental model. You're identifying friction, measuring the cost of a problem, and engineering a solution where the ROI is obvious.

## What I've learned

1. **Ship fast.** The market doesn't wait for perfect code.
2. **Solve your own problems.** The best tools come from genuine pain points.
3. **Compound knowledge.** Every project teaches you something the next one needs.

The intersection of finance and AI isn't just about fintech. It's about bringing a builder mindset to a field that desperately needs better tools.
