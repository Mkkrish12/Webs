## Episode Notes

In this first episode, I talk about:

- Why I started building AI tools instead of just studying finance
- The moment I realized I could solve my own problems with code
- How multi-agent systems are changing what solo builders can create
- What I'm working on next and where this podcast is headed

## Key Takeaway

You don't need permission to build. The tools are available, the knowledge is free, and the best time to start is now.
